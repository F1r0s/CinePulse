Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your information.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1 Introduction

Welcome to CinePulse ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you visit our website at cinepulse.live (the "Service"). By using CinePulse, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

CinePulse is a free streaming index service. We do not require registration or account creation. We do not store user accounts, passwords, or payment information.

2 Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate and improve our service:

🌐

Usage Data

Pages visited, time spent, browser type, device type, and referring URL — collected automatically via server logs.

🔍

Search Queries

Search terms you type into our search bar, used to improve search results and content recommendations.

📍

Approximate Location

General geographic region (country/city) derived from your IP address. We never collect precise GPS location.

🍪

Cookies

Session cookies and local storage for preferences (e.g., last-watched content). No persistent tracking cookies.

3 How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Operate and maintain the CinePulse website.
  • Improve our content recommendations and search functionality.
  • Analyze usage trends to enhance user experience.
  • Monitor and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4 Third-Party Services

CinePulse uses the following third-party services which may collect data independently:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — used to fetch movie and TV show metadata, posters, and ratings.
  • Videasy — the video embed player used to stream content. Videasy has its own privacy policy and may collect playback data.
  • Google Fonts — used to serve typography. Google may log requests for fonts.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party providers. We have no control over their data practices.

5 Cookies & Tracking

CinePulse uses minimal cookies. These include:

  • Session storage: Used to remember your current session preferences (e.g., selected category tab).
  • Local storage: May store recently viewed titles for a better browsing experience.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Doing so may affect some functionality of the site.

6 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to deletion: Request that we delete your personal data.
  • Right to opt-out: Opt out of any data collection beyond what is strictly necessary.
  • Right to portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

7 Children's Privacy

CinePulse is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.
